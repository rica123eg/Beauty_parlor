Your comprehensive information system solution for modern salon appointments, client profiling, and service management.

Project Overview

The Beauty Parlor Management System is a centralized digital solution that streamlines daily salon operations. It enables customers to book beauty services online while allowing staff to manage schedules, customer records, service availability, and transactions. Ultimately, it reduces paperwork, minimizes booking errors, improves customer service, and gives management accurate data for better decision-making.

System Objectives
  • Automate appointment booking and queue management.
  • Maintain secure customer beauty profiles and service histories.
  • Manage product inventory and salon equipment tracking.
  • Provide real-time dashboards and sales analytics for management.
